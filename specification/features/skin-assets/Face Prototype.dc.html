Changed from rev 1
Faces are sprites, not primitives — one 320×240 body plate per skin, one shared 19-plate feature set tinted to the skin's ink. Seven emotions: calm joined the table. Lip-sync keeps rev 1's motion — the emotion mouth stretched by the envelope — and gains the four viseme plates as an alternative you can switch to. Gaze is a ±14 px blit offset on both feature rects, not a ±10 px SVG translate on the eyes alone. Chrome bands are drawn, so it is visible what the face may never occupy.
Dropped, on purpose
The SVG glows, blurs and gradient stacks are gone: on RGB565 they band, and the firmware has no filter stage. Per-skin colour logic is gone too — flame, jelly and cloud are one neutral plate multiplied by a colour from the manifest, which is what keeps a new skin from needing renderer code. Idle bob is out while the body is a single plate: bobbing it would drag the sky with it.
Still true to the contract
renderFace(skin, frame) stays pure — same frame, same pixels. The device never decides an emotion; blink and lip-sync are the only motion it originates. Assets and anchors come from manifest.json; the plates are on the asset sheet.
