RoboFace — device UI prototype

The chrome around the face on the Core S3's 320×240 screen. The face expresses state; chrome expresses only facts a face cannot say — signal, charge, a live camera, what a gesture just did. Geometry here is device geometry: face 264×184 in the centre, chrome in the outer 28 px, and a single-tenant bottom band. Spec: features/DEVICE_UI.md.

Press and hold on the screen to try PTT → hold past 1.2 s in silence and it becomes the skin carousel. Drag while the carousel is open, release to confirm.

Level meter, toast and carousel never share it. When two want it: carousel > toast > level. The band is 28 px; nothing ever draws over the face.

Never notify what the character can say (a greeting, an answer, a result), and never notify a state the face already shows. A toast is for facts the face has no way to express.
