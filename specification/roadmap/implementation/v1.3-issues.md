# v1.3 — GitHub Issues

Issues for phase **v1.3 — ASR and the full voice loop** (version **v1 — Voice**),
derived from the per-phase Tasks in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) (§v1.3) and the
contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) (§Contracts, §Audio pipeline, §Turn lifecycle,
§Budgets and abort semantics).
This file is scoped to a single phase; IDs continue from the previous phase
(RF-040 → **RF-041…RF-047**).

The loop closes. v1.2 sends audio while you speak; this recognises it while you speak, so that when
you stop the transcript is **already in hand** and the ASR stage collapses to nearly nothing. That
one choice — a WebSocket to Deepgram rather than a batch POST — is most of the latency budget:
batch recognition pays its ~1.4 s *after* the utterance ends, where the person is waiting.

**Reconciled.** `asr_partial` and `asr` are declared in `ServerMessage` (v0.1) and the six
`DEEPGRAM_*` variables are already in `server/.env`. The `ASRProvider` seam is *named* in
`providers/base.py`'s prose and does not exist as a type.

**Carried from v1.2, and each changes something here:**

1. **`endListening` ends the turn in the firmware** — a line added in v1.2 with a comment saying
   v1.3 deletes it. A reply follows an utterance now, so the turn must stay open. RF-046 removes it.
2. **The utterance is released when a socket drops.** ASR must not assume a partial utterance
   survives a reconnection.
3. **An empty utterance is valid.** A very short hold produces zero bytes, and this phase must treat
   that as "nothing was said" rather than as a fault.

## v1.3 — ASR and the full voice loop

### RF-041 — The `ASRProvider` seam and its scripted mock

**Description:**
The third vendor seam. Unlike the other two it is **fed** as well as read: audio goes in while
transcripts come out, so the shape is a session rather than a call. Touches
`server/roboface_server/providers/{base,mock}.py`, `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md` and
`tests/contract/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `ASRChunk{text, is_final}` and an `ASRProvider` Protocol: open a session, **push** audio frames
  into it, and read `AsyncIterator[ASRChunk]` out of it.
- Push and read are separate directions of one session, which is what makes recognition concurrent
  with speech. A seam shaped as `transcribe(audio) -> str` would make that impossible and is the
  shape this phase exists to avoid.
- A mock that replays **scripted events** — interims, a punctuated final, an un-punctuated final,
  and an `UtteranceEnd` — so RF-042's rules can be tested against sequences a real vendor produces
  without a network.
- `ProviderError` carrying `ErrorCode.ASR_FAILED`, which already exists.
- **Seam change:** `ARCHITECTURE.md` §Providers and seams gains the third signature, in this issue,
  with its contract test.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:** A pinned `ASRProvider` seam the rest of the phase is built against.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Audio can be pushed while chunks are being read — proven by a test that interleaves them
- [ ] The mock replays a scripted sequence, including a final with no terminal punctuation
- [ ] **Contract test:** the session shape, and that a failure carries `asr_failed`
- [ ] `ARCHITECTURE.md` records the seam, in this commit
- [ ] **No paid API:** nothing here calls Deepgram

### RF-042 — `utterance.py` — the tracker, and the phrase-hold

**Description:**
The rule that decides when a person has actually finished speaking. Pure — no provider, no
protocol, no clock of its own. Touches `server/roboface_server/utterance.py` and `tests/unit/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- Consume `ASRChunk`s and assemble the utterance: interims replace, finals accumulate.
- **The phrase-hold.** A `speech_final` that ends without terminal punctuation is a **breathing
  pause mid-sentence**, not the end of a thought. It is *held* — for the continuation that follows,
  or for the `UtteranceEnd` backstop if none comes. Answering it immediately is how a character
  interrupts someone mid-sentence, which reads as rudeness rather than as a bug.
- The `UtteranceEnd` backstop releases whatever is held, so a held phrase can never wait forever.
- An **empty utterance** resolves to nothing rather than to an error — v1.2's press-and-hold can
  produce one and this phase must not treat it as a fault.
- Pure and total: every sequence of events resolves, and the tests drive it with the scripted
  fixtures rather than a live vendor.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:** A tracker that answers a finished thought and waits through a pause.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Interims replace rather than accumulate; finals accumulate
- [ ] An un-punctuated final is held, then joined with its continuation
- [ ] A held phrase is released by the `UtteranceEnd` backstop
- [ ] A punctuated final resolves at once
- [ ] An empty utterance resolves to nothing, not an error
- [ ] **Unit test:** the above against scripted Deepgram-shaped sequences

### RF-043 — `providers/deepgram.py` — recognition over a WebSocket

**Description:**
The real adapter. Touches `server/roboface_server/providers/deepgram.py`, `config.py`, `tests/unit/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- The `wss://` listen URL: `linear16`, 16 kHz, mono, Ukrainian, `interim_results=true`,
  `smart_format=true`, server-side `endpointing` (~500 ms) and `utterance_end_ms` (~1000 ms).
- **The URL builder is its own testable function.** Every one of those parameters changes behaviour
  and none of them fail loudly when wrong — a missing `interim_results` simply produces no interims,
  which looks like a slow network.
- **The connector is injectable**, so tests drive a fake socket. Without that, every test of this
  adapter is a paid network call and none of them run in CI.
- Config through `config.py`, following the `GEMINI_*` and `ELEVENLABS_*` pattern;
  `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` redacted in `Settings.__repr__` like the other two.
- Failures map to `asr_failed`, hinting only what is unambiguous — the same policy the other two
  adapters follow, and for the same reason.

**Dependencies:** RF-041

**Expected result:** Audio pushed to Deepgram returns interims and finals as they are recognised.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The URL carries every documented parameter — pinned by a test on the builder alone
- [ ] Audio pushed mid-session produces chunks without waiting for the session to close
- [ ] `repr(Settings)` redacts `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`
- [ ] Errors map to `asr_failed` without blaming the device
- [ ] **Unit test:** against an injected fake socket — **no paid call in the suite**

### RF-045 — The full pipeline, with a budget per stage

**Description:**
The join: audio in, speech out, three streaming stages and nothing buffered between them. Touches
`server/roboface_server/{orchestrator,router}.py` and `tests/integration/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- Audio arriving in the listening window is pushed to ASR **as it arrives**, not at `listen_stop`.
  That is the entire point: at the endpointing decision the transcript already exists.
- The resolved utterance starts the LLM stage; the existing phrase splitter and TTS path follow
  unchanged. All three stages stream, and the frames interleave on the wire.
- **A budget per stage** — ASR ≤ ~5 s, LLM first token ≤ ~8 s, TTS first audio ≤ ~3 s — each on the
  stage's *first output*, the shape already used twice. A breach aborts to that stage's own error
  code, so a person reading a log learns which leg failed.
- A device disconnect cancels the turn, including the ASR session — v1.2 already releases the
  utterance buffer, and an open vendor socket is the same problem with a bill attached.
- **Latency instrumentation:** `listen_stop → asr final`, `→ first reply delta`, `→ first tts_audio`,
  each logged as its own number. The DoD is a claim about which leg is smallest, and that cannot be
  checked without three separate numbers.

**Dependencies:** RF-042, RF-043, RF-044

**Expected result:** Speaking produces a spoken answer, with every stage streaming.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Audio is pushed to ASR during the window, not after it
- [ ] Each stage's budget is on its first output; a breach maps to that stage's error code
- [ ] A disconnect cancels the turn and closes the ASR session
- [ ] **Integration test:** `audio → tts_end` against mocks, with **interleaved** stage frames
- [ ] **Integration test:** the three failure paths, each producing its own code
- [ ] The three latency numbers are logged separately

### RF-046 — The device in a voice turn

**Description:**
The firmware's half. Touches `firmware/src/pure/ws_protocol.h`, `firmware/src/app/ws.cpp` and
`firmware/src/main.cpp`.

**What needs to be done:**
- Parse `asr_partial` and `asr`, and **add them to `ws.cpp`'s dispatch whitelist** — a `ParseResult`
  that reaches the parser and not the dispatch is discarded one layer below `main.cpp`, silently.
- Render both to the serial debug channel. **The screen still shows states, never words** — that
  rule does not bend for a transcript any more than it bent for the console in v0.5.
- **Delete the `kTurnEnded` that v1.2's `endListening` sends.** It exists because v1.2 had no reply
  to end the turn; now one arrives, and leaving it would end the turn before the answer.
- The state machine's existing `kListening → kThinking` transition is finally correct as written.

**Dependencies:** RF-044

**Expected result:** The device shows the turn's states and logs what was heard.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `asr_partial` and `asr` are parsed **and dispatched**
- [ ] Both render on serial; no transcript text reaches the screen
- [ ] The v1.2 turn-ending line is gone and a spoken turn completes on the reply
- [ ] `pio test -e native` and `pio run -e cores3` green

### RF-047 — Spoken to, and answered — the DoD and the latency table

**Description:**
The phase's claim is a number, so this measures it. Touches `TESTING.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- Speak Ukrainian to the board and be answered aloud.
- **Read the latency breakdown**: first audio should follow `listen_stop` by under ~1.5 s, and the
  **ASR leg must be the smallest of the three**. If it is the largest, recognition is running in
  batch somewhere and the phase has missed its point however well it works.
- Confirm a breathing pause mid-sentence does not trigger an early answer.
- Document the check and the three numbers in `TESTING.md`.

**Dependencies:** RF-045, RF-046

**Expected result:** The loop closes, and the numbers show why it feels quick.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Speaking Ukrainian yields a spoken answer
- [ ] First audio < ~1.5 s after `listen_stop`
- [ ] The logged ASR leg is the smallest of the three
- [ ] A mid-sentence pause does not produce an early answer
- [ ] `TESTING.md` documents the check

## v1.3 scope notes

**Total effort:** roughly 24–32 days across seven issues; two S, three M, two L.
**Critical path:** RF-041 → RF-043 → RF-045 → RF-047.
**Phase DoD (roadmap §v1.3):** speaking Ukrainian yields a spoken answer with the transcript already
resolved when the utterance ends; **first audio < ~1.5 s after `listen_stop`**, and the logged
breakdown shows the ASR leg as the *smallest* of the three; a breathing pause does not trigger an
early answer; each stage's failure maps to its own error code.
**Contracts pinned this phase:** the **`ASRProvider` seam** (RF-041) and the **`asr_partial`/`asr`
frames** (RF-044), each with its `ARCHITECTURE.md` update and contract test in the same commit.
**Model note:** chat stays **Gemini 2.5 Flash only**. ASR is Deepgram behind its own seam and TTS is
ElevenLabs behind its — speech is not chat, and three seams is the whole vendor surface. **No paid
API in the suite:** the ASR connector is injectable, and the one paid check is RF-047's manual DoD.
