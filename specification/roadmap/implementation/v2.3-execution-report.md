## The frame-rate measurement

RF-066's whole remit, and the numbers rather than the intention:

| interval | FPS while speaking | mouth changes / 10 s | `drop` / `ref` | `mic` in listening windows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 120 ms (before) | 8 | 7, 16 | 0 / 0 | 47–48 |
| 60 ms | 11 | 23, 35 | 0 / 0 | 47 |
| **40 ms (landed)** | **20** | **88** | **0 / 0** | **45–47** |

Two intermediate values were tried rather than one, because *"the interval is the binding
constraint"* needed establishing: the per-push cost could as easily have been the ceiling. It was the
interval. Half-duplex suspends the microphone during playback, so these frames compete with nothing —
a prediction until it was measured, and this project has twice found the opposite.

## The finding that mattered most, and it was not in an issue

**A fixture that does not match the signal produces numbers that pass every test and fail on the
desk.** This phase demonstrated it twice, in the same afternoon, on the same table.

RF-064 replaced the peak detector with an RMS envelope, correctly. RF-065 then calibrated the viseme
ladder against that envelope's distribution — measured from a synthetic fixture, which spanned
0.067..0.675 and gave clean quintiles. Every test passed, including the one written specifically to
catch an unreachable band: *"every rung is reached by real speech."*

Then RF-066 put it on the board, which reported `lvl=0..38%`.

A sine peaks at 1.4× its RMS; speech peaks at three to four times, because most of a voiced block is
far quieter than its glottal pulses. So the fixture carried roughly twice the energy of the thing it
modelled, and thresholds derived from it put the top two rungs out of the device's reach entirely:
`mouth=7` and `mouth=16` per ten seconds — **worse than the peak detector the phase had just
removed.**

The fixture now has speech's crest factor, spans 0.034..0.341, matches the board, and the ladder
sits on its quintiles: `mouth=88`.

Worth stating plainly, because the shape of the mistake is more useful than the mistake: the test
asserting "every rung is used" was correct, well-motivated, and passed against a ladder whose top two
rungs the device could never reach. It was asking the right question of the wrong signal. The same
error in a different costume — a rule that was right being fed something wrong — is what v2.2's
review found in `mouthRuns`.

## Detailed Results

### RF-064: The RMS envelope, against real PCM
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** `197b0d3`
**Validation:** [x] 12 new host tests · [x] compiles · [x] `src/pure/` stays Arduino-free

`pure/envelope.h`, separate from `level.h` because a mouth and a meter answer different questions and
the constants have already been tuned apart once. `peakLevel` is untouched and still drives the input
meter; `level.h`'s "peak, not RMS" note now says which of the two it is arguing for.

Two defects came out of writing the tests. Silence returned `2.9e-11` — Newton's method halves toward
the root and never arrives, and from a seed of 1.0 that is where twenty iterations land: a number
that is not silence, sits above no threshold, and would have stayed invisible until something
compared an envelope against zero. And the first burst-train fixture alternated loud and quiet every
single 32 ms chunk, four times faster than any syllable; the smoothing correctly flattened it and the
test correctly failed.

### RF-065: The viseme bands, calibrated to the envelope
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** `e1174be`, corrected in `3087e87`
**Validation:** [x] 3 new host tests · [x] ARCHITECTURE updated

The tests assert the **property**, not the numbers: every rung reached, none holding more than 60% of
the frames, at least two changes per syllable. That is what makes re-tuning free and breaking the
ladder expensive — and it is why the correction in RF-066 was a change of constants rather than a
change of design.

## Architecture impact

No seam moved. `EmotionFrame` already carries `speaking` and deliberately carries no level, and this
phase is the strongest form of that claim: **the server sends nothing whatsoever for the mouth.**

`ARCHITECTURE.md` gained **§The viseme ladder** — the table is the definition, the envelope is RMS
while the input meter keeps its peak, and the thresholds are calibrated against a measured
distribution rather than chosen. It also records that roadmap §v2.3's "four bands" was an estimate
made before there was anything to measure; the table has five and the table is authoritative.
