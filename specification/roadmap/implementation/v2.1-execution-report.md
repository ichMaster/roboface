## Detailed results

### RF-054 — The layer bank
`pure/layers.h`: six layers in composite order, `layout()` total over every recipe. Nothing leaves a
float for the renderer to interpret — eye openness arrives as a height, a brow angle as two
endpoints, the mouth as three control points. **`dim` is one brightness for the whole bank**,
because dimming layers separately lets a tired face come apart into differently-lit pieces. Out-of-
range recipes clamp: a recipe interpolated mid-crossfade can briefly sit outside 0..1, and a face
that inverts for one frame is extremely visible.

### RF-055 — The idle loop
`pure/idle.h`: blink, breathe, gaze drift, as offsets over the bank. Randomised **but
deterministic** — a fixed interval reads as a metronome, an unseeded random is untestable, and a
seeded xorshift gives both. `intensity` scales all three without the loop knowing what an emotion
is, which keeps v2.2 an addition. A triangle wave rather than a sine: `sinf` is a libm call on every
frame, and at three pixels over four seconds nobody can tell them apart.

### RF-056 — Crossfade
`pure/crossfade.h`: eased, not linear — a linear blend reads as a slide rather than a change of
expression. **An interrupted fade restarts from where the face is**, which is the case that happens
constantly in practice (idle → listening → thinking inside a second) and never in a demo. `ttl_ms`
relaxation drifts home over 900 ms against a 200 ms state change, because relaxing at the same speed
as deciding reads as another decision.

### RF-057 — `ProceduralRenderer`
Implements the existing `IFaceRenderer` — **the seam is unchanged**, exactly as v0.4 intended when
it fixed the interface against a stub. Composites into one PSRAM sprite and pushes once, so the DoD
can say "no tearing" and mean it. A settled face costs nothing: `tick` returns early when nothing
moved, and the device is idle most of the time. A closed eye is drawn as a *line* — `fillRoundRect`
with no height draws nothing, so a blink would make the eyes vanish rather than close. `StubRenderer`
deleted, not left unused.

### RF-058 — The frame budget
**`fps=15` sustained**, steady across intervals, inside the 15–20 the roadmap asks for. Repaint
interval 55 ms rather than the stub's 33 — deliberately below what the panel could manage.

**Architecture impact:** none to any contract. One convention worth carrying: the status line now
reports the face's frame rate and the capture rate **together**, because either alone hides the
trade. A face at 18 FPS that cost a third of the audio looks healthy in one number and is a
regression in the other, and that is exactly how it went unnoticed three times in v1.

## Two faults found while adding the measurement

Both are the same shape as bugs this project has already paid for, which is the point of recording
them rather than just fixing them.

- **A `printf` format string gained two placeholders and no arguments**, so it walked off the end of
  its list and the board panicked in a loop. The backtrace lands in `_svfprintf_r`, which is not
  where anyone looks first; decoding it with `addr2line` took a minute and guessing would have taken
  much longer.
- **`frames - baseline` underflowed to four billion** because the capture tally resets at every
  window. Unsigned subtraction against a counter someone else resets is worth distrusting on sight —
  the listening-window cap in v1.4 made exactly this mistake and cost hours.
